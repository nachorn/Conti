Help with playing together

Create a room and invite friends

  1. Choose a game on the home page. Enter the name your table will see. For Continental, choose two or three decks; Pocha’s settings are chosen inside the room.
  2. Share the invitation. Send your friends the room link, or give them its four-digit code to enter under “Join” on the home page.
  3. Wait until everyone is connected. Each participant plays from their own browser. Once there are at least two players and the table is ready, the host starts.

Share the link only with people you want to invite. The code identifies the room but is not a private password. Rooms are not a public list with automatic matchmaking against strangers.

Before starting, read the Continental or Pocha guide. They describe this website’s actual rules, including buying discards, using Jokers and playing auction rounds.

Save a game and return

Rooms you join appear under “My games” on the home page. Return with the same browser and profile, then choose “Return to room” to recover your seat. No email account is needed.

When leaving a game, “Save and exit” keeps your seat and cards. If the host does this, the game pauses for everyone. Another player keeps their place while others may continue. In a paused game, the host can continue once everyone has returned.

Rooms are kept for 30 days of inactivity. They are not an indefinite backup: rooms may be removed earlier after permanent departures or during cleanup of rooms without connected players.

Keep your browser’s access details

Clearing browser data, switching profiles or closing a private browsing session can lose the key to your seat. An invitation link or the same player name alone does not restore a previous seat. “Forget” removes that device’s access from the saved list.

“Leave permanently” is different from saving: you lose your seat and cards, and the game may end or reset for the others. To continue another day, use “Save and exit”.

If something goes wrong

I cannot create or join a room

Check your Internet connection and wait for the website to connect to the table. Enter a name when creating a room. To join, check that the code has four digits. If an access check appears, complete it before continuing. A closed, full or already-started room may not accept new players.

My connection dropped

Open Conti in the same browser and use “My games”. A short disconnection is not a permanent departure. If the table is paused, each player must recover their own seat before the host continues. Do not clear browser data as a way to troubleshoot the connection.

I cannot play the card I want

Check whose turn it is and whether you must first draw or respond to a discard. In Continental, you must play the full contract and cannot add cards before going down. In Pocha, following suit and beating the winning card may be compulsory; playable cards are marked in your hand. A short result display also follows each completed trick.

I found a bug

Use “Report bug” to copy a report. Review it before sharing, then email nacho.rn.2000@gmail.com with the game, steps you took and what you expected. The button does not send an email automatically. Keep access keys and sensitive information out of public screenshots.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost money to play?

Play is currently free. The support link on the home page goes to Football Champion on Buy Me a Coffee for voluntary contributions to the project. A contribution does not buy an in-game advantage.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Use a phone, tablet or desktop browser without installing an application. In Continental, sort your hand by rank or suit and move cards to organise them. In Pocha, select an available card and confirm the action.

Can I continue on another device?

A saved seat belongs to the browser you used. Player accounts do not currently sync that access across devices. Keep the original browser to resume; sharing a room code alone does not transfer your seat.

How many people can play?

Both games allow two to ten players. Three decks are advisable for Continental tables of more than five. Pocha’s maximum hand size depends on the selected deck and number of players.

Where are the rules and project information?

Read the Continental guide, Pocha guide and About Conti. The privacy notice explains game data, and the terms describe use of the service.
