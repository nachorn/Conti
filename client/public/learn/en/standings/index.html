How your league ranking works

TrueSkill works out your league rating from the games you play. Each league has its own standings.

Why does my rating go up or down?

It depends on where you finished, who you played and whether anyone tied. Finishing ahead of a favourite usually helps more than beating a lower-rated player. With the same opponents and the same finishing order, winning by 1 point or 50 counts the same.

TrueSkill estimates your level and subtracts a margin for uncertainty. At first it knows little about you. More games usually shrink that margin, so your rating can rise even when you don’t win.

When do I get a place?

Each league sets a minimum number of games per player. Your rating moves from the first game. Until you meet the minimum, you’re provisional.

The highest rating goes first. If players have the same rating, more wins come first, followed by the higher win percentage. If all three are equal, they share the place.

For a game to count, the host picks the league before starting and everyone finishes on their member account, without bots or anyone leaving.
