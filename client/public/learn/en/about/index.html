What is Conti?

Conti is an independent project created by Ignacio Rodriguez Novas Catala. It lets you organise Continental and Pocha games with people you invite: create a room, share its link and play together from your browsers.

The table deals cards, checks moves and keeps score. Players make the decisions: what to keep in Continental, when to buy a discard or how many tricks to predict in Pocha. The aim is to keep the choices and conversation of a card game while the website handles the arithmetic.

Play in English or Spanish on a phone, tablet or desktop. You do not need to install an application or create an account to enter a room. Saved games can be recovered from the same browser through “My games”.

Two ways to play

Continental

A rummy-family game of matching melds. The trios and straights needed to go down change over seven rounds. Empty your hand and avoid penalties: the lowest total wins.

Read the Continental rules →

Pocha

A trick-prediction game using a Spanish deck. The key is matching your prediction, not winning every possible trick. Play normal rounds or choose a mode with auctions at the maximum hand size.

Read the Pocha rules →

Our guides explain the variants used at this table, with worked examples. For invitations, saving and reconnecting, visit Help.

Playing and supporting the project

Play is currently free. Optional support is handled by Football Champion’s Buy Me a Coffee page, linked from the home page. Conti does not organise betting or offer cash prizes for winning a game.

Contact and suggestions

To report a bug, ask a question or suggest an improvement, email nacho.rn.2000@gmail.com. Say whether you were playing Continental or Pocha and describe what happened. If you can reproduce a problem, the steps are more helpful than an isolated screenshot.

The “Report bug” button prepares a report for you to review and share. It does not send it automatically. Avoid sending sensitive information or publishing private access details for a room.
